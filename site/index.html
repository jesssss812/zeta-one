THE PRINCIPLE BEHIND THE NAME

Enough control.
Room to move.

In control theory, ζ = 1 is critical damping: a return to stability without overshoot. In agentic finance, it’s a design principle—match the intervention to the risk.

Allow

Let legitimate, low-risk actions proceed.

Challenge

Ask for the verification the uncertainty warrants.

Block

Enforce hard boundaries and stop clear threats.

04 / A CLOSED FEEDBACK LOOP

Every correction
can make the next decision better.

When a reviewer corrects a decision, the verified outcome becomes feedback—not a note left behind. The learning cycle is designed to carry that feedback into evaluation cases and proposed improvements to risk rules and models.

Automate the cycle around human judgment: capture the correction and its provenance, evaluate the change, and monitor its effect. People retain control of consequential decisions and policy changes.
