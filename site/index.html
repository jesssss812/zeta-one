02 / INTELLIGENCE, ALLOCATED

Spend intelligence
where it matters.

A frontier model on every payment carries a cost. So does asking a person to review every legitimate action.

01
DETERMINISTIC CHECKS

Enforce the
boundaries.

Verify the agent and check scope, limits, expiry, and revocation. Hard constraints are enforced in code; a model cannot override them.

02
JEV, WHERE IT FITS

Assess the
context.

Use targeted, structured risk decisions instead of a long generated response for every payment. Jev is the intended inexpensive classification tier.

03
SELECTIVE ESCALATION

Go deeper
selectively.

Reserve stronger reasoning for cases that need it. Match human verification to the uncertainty, keeping consequential review decisions with people.

Design intent. Cost savings, calibration, and decision quality will be tested on the same labeled scenarios.

THE PRINCIPLE BEHIND THE NAME

Enough control.
Room to move.

In control theory, ζ = 1 is critical damping: a return to stability without overshoot. In agentic finance, it’s a design principle—match the intervention to the risk.

Allow

Let legitimate, low-risk actions proceed.

Challenge

Ask for the verification the uncertainty warrants.

Block

Enforce hard boundaries and stop clear threats.
